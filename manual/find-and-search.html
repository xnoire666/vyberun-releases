Find and search

Two searches: inside the tab you're in, and everywhere.

Find in a tab

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+F. The find bar opens over the tab row.
  2. Type what you're looking for. It searches the tab's whole history, not just the screen.
  3. Enter or ↑ goes to an older match, Shift+Enter or ↓ to a newer one. Esc closes the bar.

The bar's Match case button makes upper and lower case count.

Find in a Claude Code conversation

Claude Code redraws its screen, so most of a long conversation isn't in the terminal any more. In a Claude tab, Ctrl+Shift+F searches Claude's whole conversation instead:

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+F in the Claude tab and type.
  2. Press Enter. vyberun shows the conversation with the matches, even what's long gone from the screen.
  3. Esc goes back to Claude.

Search everywhere

The search box in the title bar searches two things. Tab switches between them.

  • Files: files and folders on your PC, by name: what Windows has indexed, plus your projects' folders. Results show as you type.
  • vyberun: your tabs, cards, servers, snippets, commands, settings and Help's pages.

Press Ctrl+Shift+P to go to it from anywhere.

In the results

KeysDoes
EnterOpens it: a file in its app, a folder in File Explorer, a tab or card in vyberun. On a setting, switches it.
Shift+EnterFiles and folders: opens a new terminal there
Ctrl+EnterFiles: shows it in File Explorer
EscLeaves the search

Search tips

  • Words match in any order: checkout request finds CheckoutRequest.php.
  • * or % stands for anything in between: cust%test finds CustomerTest.php, report*2024 finds report-march-2024.pdf.
  • Windows doesn't index some folders, such as AppData. Add them in Settings ▸ Search ▸ Also search these folders and vyberun searches them too.

Recent cards

Ctrl+Shift+R lists your cards, the most recent first. Hold Ctrl+Shift, tap R to go down the list, and let go to open the card.
