Manual › The terminal

Screenshots

Turn what's in a terminal into a picture, mark it up and share it.

A screenshot of some lines

  1. Highlight the lines.
  2. Press Ctrl+Shift+I.

The picture is copied, and its preview opens so you can mark it up.

A long screenshot

For more than fits on the screen: drag past the bottom of the window (it scrolls as you go), then press Ctrl+Shift+K. Everything you highlighted becomes one picture.

In Claude Code

Claude Code redraws its screen, so there are two ways:

Only what's on Claude's screen, with collapsed output left collapsed:

  1. Highlight a line or two where the picture should start, and press Ctrl+Shift+K. The start is marked.
  2. Scroll to where it should end, highlight a line or two there, and press Ctrl+Shift+K again.
  3. Wait while the screen is dimmed: vyberun scrolls Claude's view from one to the other and joins it.

To take just what's highlighted on the screen, press Ctrl+Shift+K twice without highlighting anything new in between.

Everything, with the commands' full output:

  1. With nothing highlighted, press Ctrl+Shift+I. Screenshot mode puts the whole conversation in the terminal, even what Claude's screen hides.
  2. Highlight any part, as long as you like.
  3. Enter takes it. Esc goes back to Claude without one.

In a server tab

In a server tab, Ctrl+Shift+I with nothing highlighted brings the tab's whole history from the server, so you can highlight past the screen. This works when the server keeps it with tmux. A server you sign in to with a password can't be asked this way: hold Shift and drag to highlight what's on screen.

Marking it up

In the preview:

KeyTool
PPen
HHighlighter
RBox
OCircle
EEraser
CCrop: drag around the part that matters
Ctrl+ZUndo the last mark or crop

To look around a picture larger than the window, hold V or Ctrl and drag. Ctrl+=, Ctrl+-, Ctrl+0 and Ctrl+1 zoom in, zoom out, fit the window and show it at actual size.

Copying and saving

  • Ctrl+C copies the picture with your marks. Paste it anywhere, or straight into Claude Code.
  • Ctrl+S saves it as a PNG file.
  • Esc closes the preview.

Settings ▸ Screenshots ▸ Frame the screenshots puts a soft frame and shadow around them. The preview's Framed and Plain switch does the same for one picture.
