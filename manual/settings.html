Settings

What each page of vyberun's Settings holds.

Open Settings with File ▸ Settings… or Ctrl+,. You can also find any setting from the search: Ctrl+Shift+P, Tab to vyberun, and type its name. Enter on a setting switches it.

Each page has Reset to defaults, for that page only. Changes apply at once; there's nothing to save.
