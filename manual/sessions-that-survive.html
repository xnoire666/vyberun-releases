Sessions that survive

Close vyberun and your tabs keep running. Open it again and they're back, further along.

Closing vyberun

vyberun's tabs don't run inside its window. Each tab's shell runs in a separate background program, the session host, so closing the window doesn't stop them. A build, a server or a Claude Code session keeps going while vyberun is closed.

  1. Close vyberun. If tabs are open, it asks what to do with them: Keep running, Stop everything or Cancel.
  2. Choose Keep running. Your tabs carry on in the background.
  3. Open vyberun again, or click its icon in the notification area of the taskbar. Your tabs come back with everything they printed meanwhile.

Restart vyberun in the File menu always keeps the tabs.

Choosing what closing does

Tick Remember my choice in the close dialog, or open Settings ▸ Closing vyberun and pick one:

ChoiceWhen vyberun closes with tabs open
Ask each timevyberun asks (the default).
Keep them running in the backgroundThe window closes; every tab keeps running.
Stop everythingEvery tab's program is stopped, then vyberun closes.

Stopping ends running programs the normal way first, as Ctrl+C would, so nothing is cut off in the middle of writing a file.

What's still running

While vyberun is closed, its icon in the notification area says how many tabs run in the background. Click it to open vyberun. Right-click it for Open vyberun or Stop everything….

While vyberun is open, the memory and CPU next to the tabs count vyberun and everything in its tabs. They turn amber when this PC runs low on memory. Click them, or choose Terminal ▸ Activity…, to see each tab's share and stop what you don't need.

Putting a tab away

To clear the tab row without stopping a tab, detach it. It keeps running inside vyberun, out of view.

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+X in the tab.
  2. To bring it back, click the small arrow next to + above the terminal and choose it, or hold Ctrl and right-click a tab ▸ Re-attach tab.

What ends a session

Tabs keep running until one of these happens:

  • Windows restarts, shuts down or signs you out.
  • You stop them: Stop everything, closing a tab, or Stop in Activity.
  • The program in the tab exits, for example when you type exit.
  • The Store updates vyberun (see Updating and uninstalling).

Warning. A restart for Windows Update ends your tabs too. Save your work before one.

On a server

A server tab's work can keep running on the server itself, even with your PC off: close the tab with Leave it running on the server, then rejoin it later with ▶ on the server's card. This uses the server's own tmux; nothing is installed.
