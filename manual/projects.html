Projects

Any folder can be a project, with its own tabs and commands.

Adding a project

  1. Click + next to General, or next to one of your spaces' names to add it straight there.
  2. Choose Add folder… and pick the folder.

Or drag the folder from File Explorer onto the sidebar. Settings ▸ Projects ▸ Add folder does the same.

What a card shows

  • Its type beside its name, such as node, laravel or .net, found from the files in the folder.
  • Its git branch under it.
  • Its tabs, each with its role and when you last worked in it.

With Settings ▸ Sidebar ▸ Compact cards on, a project takes one line; its branch and folder show when you hover its name.

Running its commands

You don't have to type a project's usual commands:

  1. Click ⋯ on the card ▸ Run.
  2. Choose a command. vyberun lists what it found in the folder: npm scripts, artisan, dotnet, Python and more.

It runs in a new tab of that card.

Opening it elsewhere

⋯ on the card has:

  • Open in your editor: VS Code, Cursor, PhpStorm and others. Choose which in Settings ▸ Projects ▸ Open projects in. The same editor opens files you Ctrl+click in the terminal.
  • Open Folder: File Explorer.
  • Open in Terminal: a new tab in the folder.

Several folders in one card

When folders belong together, such as an API and its web front end, click ⋯ on one card ▸ Combine with…, tick the others and give the card a name. They show as one card, whose tabs can be in any of its folders, and its Open in offers each folder or all of them. This is only in vyberun: each folder stays where it is.

Removing a project

⋯ on the card ▸ Remove from list takes it out of vyberun. The folder and its files stay where they are. See Spaces and cards for everything else a card can do.
