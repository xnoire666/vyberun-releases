Menus

Every item in vyberun's menu bar, and where it's explained.

The menus are along the top of the window. With Settings ▸ Title bar ▸ Show the full menu bar off, they open from the vyberun logo instead.

Right-click menus

  • A tab: Rename tab, Color, ✳ Set role…, Show to an agent, Let an agent investigate, ✳ Move Claude to…, ✳ Agent messages…, Group tabs, Split, Detach, Close, and Terminal settings…. A server's tab adds ⚡ Snippets and Leave it running on the server.
  • Inside a terminal: Ctrl+right-click (or right-click, if you changed it) for copy, paste, split, screenshot, Show selection to an agent, and copying files to or from a server.
  • A space's name: Rename…, Color, Archive space, Delete space.
  • ⋯ on a card: see Spaces and cards.
